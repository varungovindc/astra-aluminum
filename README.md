# Astra Shine Interactive

Act as an expert frontend developer and UI/UX designer. Create a modern, sleek, and highly animated corporate single-page website for "Astra Group of Companies" (comprising Astra Aluminium and Glass Works & Astra Smart Technical Services LLC). The design must feel premium, industrial yet high-tech, and prioritize bold typography and smooth interactions.

Design System & Typography

Colors: The primary brand colors must be extracted from the uploaded logo. Use the vibrant Orange and energetic Light Blue as primary accent colors. Use deep charcoal/black, sleek greys, and clean white for backgrounds to maintain a modern look.

Typography: All text should be big, bold, and highly legible.

Headings & Display Text: Use the font 29LT Bukra (load this custom font or use the closest available Google Font fallback like Outfit or Syncopate if strictly necessary, but enforce the bold, geometric look of the logo).

Body Text: Use Montserrat.

Animations & Interactions (CRITICAL) Implement advanced, buttery-smooth animations using Framer Motion (or equivalent):

Hero Section: Implement a text-typing animation for the main headlines.

Scroll Animations: Elements must fade in and gently slide up as they enter the viewport.

Pop-up Elements: Use scale-in (pop-up) animations for service cards or key metric highlights as the user scrolls.

Scroll-Linked Color Changes: Implement a dynamic scroll effect where specific large typography changes color (e.g., from grey to brand Blue or Orange) progressively as the scroll pointer moves down the page.

Page Structure & Content Integration

1. Hero Section (Slider)

Create a full-screen hero slider with 2 to 3 slides.

Slide 1: "Astra Group of Companies" with a typing effect highlighting "Aluminium & Glass Solutions".

Slide 2: Showcasing "Advanced Cladding & Facades".

Slide 3: Showcasing "Smart Technical Services".

Include a strong call-to-action (CTA) button on each slide.

2. About Us / Welcome

Merge the "Welcome Message" and "Who Are We?" content into a sleek, split-layout section.

Highlight key facts: Established in 2023, offices in Dubai and UAQ.

Include the message from Sankaranarayanan (Managing Director). Use large, bold pull quotes for: "We believe that every project deserves the highest standards of craftsmanship, safety, and professionalism."

3. Core Services (Grid with Hover Effects)

Create a modern masonry or strict grid layout for the services. When hovered, cards should elevate and feature an orange/blue glowing border or accent line.

List to include: Curtain wall systems, Glass works, Aluminium/Glass Doors & Windows, Frameless glass doors, Aluminium cladding (ACP & Solid panel, Stainless Steel), Ventilated facade systems, Thermal break profiles, Interior glass partitions, Glass ceramic printing, Shower cubicles, Skylights, Gypsum Works, Painting, False Ceiling, Electromechanical installation, Plumbing & Sanitary works.

4. Quality Policy

Design a dedicated, impactful section with a darker background to make it stand out.

Highlight the vision: "To be the company of first choice for all stakeholders... lean and agile in adopting work processes."

5. Footer

Clean, modern footer containing the company names: Astra Aluminium and Glass Works (Umm Al Quwain) & Astra Smart Technical Services LLC (Dubai).

Include placeholders for social media links and contact information.
